How to Play Lights Out

The goal is to turn all the lights ON (colored).

Clicking on a light toggles it and its adjacent lights (up, down, left, right).

Each move increases your move count. Try to solve the puzzle in as few moves as possible!

Choose different difficulty levels from the main menu.
